Welcome, chrononaut, to **Cubliminal** — not merely a Minecraft modification, but a philosophical descent into the layered subconscious of reality itself. While many mods seek to add content, this project seeks to *unmake* content, creating a void that paradoxically becomes the most compelling space you will ever explore. Inspired by the infinite, liminal architecture of community lore, Cubliminal is a narrative engine disguised as a dimension pack, a storybook that writes itself as you step deeper into its forgotten hallways.

## 🔍 What is Cubliminal? A Unique Proposition

Most "backrooms" or atmospheric mods give you a single, spooky dimension. They are, if you will, a one-trick pony. Cubliminal, in stark contrast, is a **multi-chaptered, interconnected multiverse of unease**.

We have taken the community's most beloved concepts—Level 0, Level 1, the entities, the inexplicable physics—and woven them into a cohesive campaign structure. Here, progress is not measured in diamonds or XP, but in *Understanding*. As you travel deeper into the "Echo Chamber" (our term for the over-arching dimension), you will uncover fragments of a lost civilization, decipher cryptic audio logs, and piece together the reason *why* this place exists.

This is a mod about **exploration, storytelling, and the tension of the unknown**. It is designed for the player who finds joy in piecing together a narrative through environmental cues rather than a quest log. It is for the architect of their own fear, the cartographer of the uncanny.

### Key Features 🎯

This is not a simple content dump. Cubliminal has been engineered with a focus on **atmospheric fidelity** and **mechanical depth**. Here are the pillars of the experience:

- **The Chapter System (Chapters 0-7):** A structured progression through distinct "Levels" or zones, each with unique biomes, block palettes, soundscapes, and rules. You cannot simply jump to the end; you must *experience* the journey to unlock the next anchor point.
- **Adaptive Audio Engine:** Sound isn't just background noise. The mod listens to your actions and location. Running in a corridor triggers a rising hum; standing still for too long invites a faint, whispering static that gets closer. Our custom audio processing creates a **responsive soundscape** that reacts to your peril.
- **The "Liminal Physics" System:** This is our flagship mechanic. Gravity might feel lighter in certain sectors. Light behaves differently—it can pool on the floor like water, or refuse to illuminate corners. Occasionally, the very layout of a room will "glitch," rearranging itself when you look away. This isn't a visual bug; it's a feature of the reality you are in.
- **Entity Ecosystem:** Entities are not just hostile mobs. They are predators with distinct AI. Some are attracted to noise; others are drawn to *silence*. Some mimic the environment (like a chair that walks when you aren't looking), and others act as gatekeepers, requiring specific items or actions to be appeased rather than fought.
- **Narrative Artifacts:** Over 30 unique, hand-written lore items (memos, VHS tapes, torn diary pages) hidden across the dimensions. Collecting them isn't just for flavor; they provide crucial clues for solving the final puzzle.
- **Progressive Difficulty:** The mod is designed to be accessible yet challenging. The early chapters are uneasy, while the later ones push you into genuine survival horror territory.
- **"The Backtrack" Mechanic:** A unique fast-travel system that is inherently risky. To teleport to a previously visited "Safe Room," you must walk *backwards* through a door. There is always a chance you'll end up somewhere else entirely. This adds a constant layer of risk-reward to your navigation.

## 🧭 Getting Started: Your First Step into the Unknown

Before you choose to traverse the threshold, you must prepare. This is not combat-heavy, but it is *survival-of-the-psyche* heavy. You will need a keen eye for detail and a willingness to get lost.

**Prerequisites**
- A compatible Minecraft launcher (Java Edition).
- A modern shader pack is **highly recommended**. The mod's lighting system interacts beautifully with shaders to create the exact oppressive atmosphere we intend.
- A decent headset. Our audio engine is the unsung hero of the horror experience. Playing without sound is like reading a book with half the pages missing.

**The Rite of Entry**
Once you have the mod installed, you will discover a new block in your crafting grid: the **"Grey Brick of Foundation."** This is your portal key. Do not place it yet. First, find a quiet, enclosed space.

1.  **Craft the Key:** You'll need a piece of concrete, a redstone torch, and a cobweb. This combination is the mechanical catalyst to "plug" into the other frequency.
2.  **Build the Frame:** Create a 2x2 frame on a wall using any block that is *not* dirt or logs (concrete is ideal).
3.  **The Placement:** Place the Grey Brick in the center of the frame. The world will stutter. A sound of a distant, failing air conditioner will grow louder.
4.  **Step Through:** Right-click (or use your interact key) on the brick. Do not close your eyes. The transition is jarring by design, but it is the only way in.

Be warned: there is no immediate way back. You must find a **"Casual Exit"** or a **"Stabilizer"** within the Echo Chamber to return to the Overworld. You are not a visitor; you are a resident now.

## 🛠️ The Technical Underpinnings (For the Curious Mind)

Cubliminal is built on a robust architecture that handles complex reality-warping without performance catastrophe.

- **Data-Driven Dimensions:** Each chapter is not hard-coded; it's a data pack. This means we can patch and add to the lore without requiring a full mod re-download. It also allows server admins to customize the experience.
- **Embedded Light Engine:** The mod uses a custom chunk renderer for specific areas to achieve the "liquid light" and "volumetric light refusal" effects. It is optimized to use less memory than standard light engines, allowing for smoother frame rates in our dense, maze-like structures.
- **Performance-First:** We've spent hundreds of hours optimizing block states and AI pathfinding. You can run this mod comfortably on 4GB of RAM with a mid-range graphics card. We aim for a smooth 60fps so you can enjoy the smooth, fluid motion of your inevitable panic.

## 🌍 The Cubliminal Universe: A Brief Lore Primer

To truly appreciate the mod, you must understand the "why." The Echo Chamber is not Hell, nor is it a pocket dimension. It is the **subconscious residue** of a failed multiversal simulation. It is the "waste data" of creation.

- **The Directors:** The unseen entities that manage the levels. They are not malevolent, but they are bound by "The Script." They enforce the traps and the glitches because it is their function. They are the middle management of the void.
- **The Wanderers:** Players like you, who have 'noclipped' in. The mod allows you to find remnants of previous Wanderers, hinting at a shared, parallel experience.
- **The Anchorites:** The main antagonists. These are beings who have been inside for so long they have become part of the walls. They hunt to maintain the narrative's tension. They are the guardians of the story, and they do not want you to finish it.

Understanding this lore is key to solving the final puzzle and 'exiting' the simulation permanently.
